# https://www.cuemath.com/

> Learn Math Online from the Best Math Tutors for Grades K to 12 — personalized 1:1 live online math tutoring, interactive and gamified, focused on concept clarity, critical thinking, and math confidence.

## Documentation
- **MathFit**: Cuemath's branded framework for building math confidence, fluency, and problem-solving ability.
- **Personalized 1:1 Tutoring**: Every session is adapted to the student's pace, grade level, and learning needs.
- **Curriculum / Grades K to 12**: Topics span arithmetic, algebra, geometry, pre-calculus, AP calculus, and more — aligned to US Common Core standards.
- **Interactive & Gamified Platform**: LEAP platform with strategy games, puzzles, logic challenges, and mental math activities.
- **Regions / Markets**: United States, India, Australia, UAE, Canada, and 80+ countries globally.
- **Programs / Offerings**: Online math classes by grade, high school math, test prep, math tutors, after-school tutoring, math homeschooling, summer math courses.
- **Partnerships**: Tutor hiring, affiliate programs, volunteering.
